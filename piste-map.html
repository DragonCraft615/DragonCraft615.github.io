Key takeaways

Best served: Tignes Val Claret — 14 lifts and 28,600 p/h combined, anchored by Tommeuses (4,500 p/h, the single highest-capacity lift in the resort) and the Perce-Neige funicular up to the glacier.

Bottlenecks: the Grande Motte glacier has the lowest combined capacity of any area (5,200 p/h across just 4 lifts) and its only cable car up top runs at 1,100 p/h. Le Fornet's cable car is even tighter at 1,000 p/h — fast, but the least throughput of any major lift in the resort.

The Tignes ⇄ Val d'Isère link (Tovière, Funival, Col de Fresse) carries a lot of daily crossover traffic on comparatively few lifts — worth timing around if you're skiing both sides.
