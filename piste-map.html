Key takeaways

Best served: Tignes Val Claret — home to Tommeuses (4,090 p/h, the single highest-capacity lift in the resort) plus five more 3,000+ p/h high-speed chairs and the Grande Motte funicular.

Bottlenecks: the Grande Motte glacier summit is only reached by a 1,010 p/h cable car — fast, but the lowest throughput of any major lift, so the top can queue hard on a powder morning. Le Fornet is similar: a 1973 cable car (1,000 p/h) plus a scatter of small drag lifts.

The Tignes ⇄ Val d'Isère link (Tovière, Funival, Col de Fresse) is comparatively thin relative to how much traffic crosses it daily — worth timing around if you're skiing both sides.
