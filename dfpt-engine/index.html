Phase 2 + Phase 3 → Phase 4: combining two octahedral rotations

A 40-atom supercell (2×2×2 replication of the cubic cell — the minimal cell commensurate with R). At R = (½,½,½) this model has a clean triple-degenerate instability: three pure single-axis octahedral rotations, each equally unstable. "Perturb" freezes two of them in together as real displacements (the standard frozen-phonon trick — this is Phase 2 + Phase 3); "relax" runs steepest descent on a genuinely nonlinear bond energy — bonds resist stretching, not just bending, which is exactly what stabilises a large-amplitude combined tilt. Watch both rotations settle in together as the saddle point falls to a real minimum.
